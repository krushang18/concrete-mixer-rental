Frequently Asked Questions

What is a self-loading concrete mixer?

A self-loading concrete mixer is a construction machine that independently loads raw materials (aggregate, cement, water), mixes them into concrete, and discharges the finished mix — all in one vehicle. It combines loader, mixer, and discharge unit, making it ideal for remote or difficult-to-access construction sites. M/S O.C.Shah rents KYB and Ajax brand machines with 4.0–4.3 cubic metre drum capacity.

What capacity concrete mixers are available for rent?

We offer self-loading concrete mixers in 4.0 and 4.3 cubic metre drum capacities, powered by diesel engines. Our fleet includes KYB Conmat and Ajax Engineering brand machines — both industry-leading manufacturers — suited for solar power plants, RCC road works, canal lining, residential, and commercial construction projects across Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra.
